**Prototype cost and capital feasibility for the design in the wayneeffect/Photonic-Computer repository**

### Rough cost of a meaningful first prototype
A “prototype” here means the first real silicon (or TFLN) that demonstrates the core claims: Floquet-modulated nonlinear Kerr interaction, measurable phase/logic contrast, at least basic cascading or multi-photon behaviour, and ideally an early quantum-memory or intensity-dependent (Chameleon-style) effect.

**Typical 2026 cost ranges**

| Item | Low-end (shared MPW, modest area) | Realistic first useful run | Notes |
|------|-----------------------------------|---------------------------|-------|
| Silicon-photonics MPW (AIM, imec, etc.) | $30k–$90k | $80k–$200k | 25–50 mm² active or low-loss cells, 10–20 dies |
| TFLN / thin-film lithium-niobate MPW (HyperLight, CCRAFT/INO-style, etc.) | $50k–$150k | $120k–$300k | Higher performance for electro-optic / nonlinear work; still emerging |
| Design, simulation, mask prep, packaging & basic test | $50k–$150k | $150k–$400k | Includes EDA, DRC, fibre attach, characterisation |
| Lab characterisation & iteration (optics, electronics, software) | $50k–$100k | $100k–$250k | Critical for proving Floquet + Kerr + memory behaviour |
| **Total for a credible first hardware prototype** | **~$200k–$400k** | **~$400k–$1M** | Multiple MPW spins or a small dedicated short-run push it higher |

A minimal “does the physics work at all?” run can stay under $300–500k. A more serious multi-gate / memory-capable demonstrator that you can show investors or partners usually lands in the **$0.5–1.5 M** range once you include people time, packaging, and measurement.

Full custom wafer runs (not shared MPW) jump into the several-million-dollar territory and are unnecessary for the first proof.

### Can you get the capital?
Yes — at the seed / pre-seed and early Series A levels this is realistic in the current environment, provided the story and team are credible.

**What investors are funding right now (2025–2026 examples)**
- Photonic quantum or advanced photonic computing startups are raising seed rounds of $4–18 M and Series A rounds of $15–50 M+.
- Pure photonic interconnect / AI-accelerator companies have gone much higher once they have silicon and customers (Lightmatter is the extreme end).
- Government and strategic money (defence, national labs, EIC-style programmes, DARPA-related, Canadian/European quantum initiatives) is actively looking for differentiated photonic platforms.

**What makes capital obtainable for this specific design**
- Clear differentiation: Floquet time-crystal ALU + engineered nonlocal Kerr + Rainbow/Chameleon-analogue control + quantum-memory angle is not a me-too silicon-photonics matrix-vector engine.
- Existing public artefacts (the GitHub repo, simulations, conceptual architectures) already show seriousness.
- Dual-use potential (AI acceleration + analogue gravity / quantum resources) appeals to both commercial and government/strategic investors.
- The first hardware milestone is relatively capital-efficient thanks to MPW services.

**Practical capital path**
1. **$300k–$1 M (pre-seed / angel / small grant)** — enough for the first MPW spin + characterisation. Sources: angels who like deep-tech photonics, university spin-out programmes, small government grants, or a tiny friends-and-family round.
2. **$3–8 M (seed)** — funds 2–3 design iterations, a small team, packaging, and a system-level demonstrator. This is the normal range for photonic hardware seeds right now.
3. **$15–40 M (Series A)** — once you have working chips that show the key nonlinear / Floquet / memory behaviours, this becomes realistic for scaling the team and moving toward a PDK or early customer pilots.

### Bottom line
A credible first prototype is likely in the **$400k–$1.5 M** all-in range. That is well within reach of a focused seed or even a strong pre-seed + grant combination in today’s photonic/quantum funding climate.  

The capital is available if you can show:
- a tight technical milestone plan tied to the GitHub concepts,
- a small but credible team (or strong academic/foundry collaborators),
- and a clear path from “first silicon works” to the platform vision you are targeting.
