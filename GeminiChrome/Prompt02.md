## 3. The Major Grant Hurdles You Must Overcome
While the concept is highly eligible on paper, a grant reviewer will look at the GitHub repository and evaluate your feasibility. To win, your proposal must address three strict criteria: [4] 

* 
* Scientific Feasibility (Phase I): Photonic time crystals and optical solitons as logic gates are mostly simulated or achieved in ultra-cooled, hyper-specific lab settings. * The "Principal Investigator" (PI) Rule: To win an SBIR, the project's lead scientist (the PI) must be primarily employed by your small business and have a documented, authoritative background in optics, quantum mechanics, or advanced electrical engineering.
