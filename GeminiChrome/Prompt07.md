## 💰 Small Business Grant Award Amounts
If you win an SBIR or STTR grant through agencies like the NSF, DARPA, or the Department of Energy, the total initial funding across the first two foundational development phases typically ranges from $950,000 to $2,000,000+.
The phased award limits break down as follows:

* Phase I (Feasibility & Simulation): Awards typically range from $150,000 to $275,000 for a 6 to 12-month period. This phase is intended to fund the mathematical modeling, software simulations, and proving the initial physics of your photonic time crystal logic gates on paper.
* Phase II (Prototype Development): If your Phase I results successfully prove technical viability, you become eligible to compete for Phase II. These awards typically range from $750,000 to $1,800,000+ over a 24-month period. This funding is explicitly meant for building physical cleanroom prototypes and starting hardware testing.
* Phase III (Commercialization / Procurement): There is no direct grant money allocated in Phase III. Instead, this phase opens up sole-source government procurement contracts where defense primes or federal agencies can buy your finalized hardware systems directly using dedicated operational budgets.

## 🥼 Cost of Hiring a Ph.D. Research Scientist
To secure these deep-tech grants, you must budget for an expert capable of serving as your Principal Investigator (PI) or Lead Simulation Engineer. The competitive market rates for a Ph.D. specializing in integrated photonics, nonlinear optics, or metamaterials depend heavily on their experience level:

* Postdoctoral Researcher (Entry-Level Ph.D.): If you hire a recent graduate directly out of a university optics lab, expect a base salary of $65,000 to $85,000 per year. In terms of contract work or part-time consulting to help draft your initial grant proposals, standard rates range from $55 to $80 per hour. [1, 2] 
* Senior Research Scientist (Mid-Level Ph.D.): For an experienced scientist with 3–5 years of post-doc laboratory or industry cleanroom experience, the market salary ranges from $110,000 to $150,000 per year.
* Fully Burdened Employment Cost: Remember that a Ph.D.'s base salary is not your only expense. When factoring in mandatory employment taxes, comprehensive health insurance, workers' comp, and specialized hardware design software licenses (like Ansys Lumerical or Synopsis), you must multiply their base salary by roughly 1.25x to 1.35x to determine the true cost to your business.

## 💡 Strategic Funding Tip
You do not have to pay these salaries out of your own pocket. The SBIR/STTR grant is designed to pay these exact hiring costs for you.
When you write your Phase I grant proposal, you can include the Ph.D.'s calculated salary directly into your Direct Labor Budget Line. You can issue them a contingent offer letter stating: "Employment begins immediately upon the formal activation and funding of our federal Phase I award." This allows you to secure the expert talent required to win the grant without risking your own capital upfront.
