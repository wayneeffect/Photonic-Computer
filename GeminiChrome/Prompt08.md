* "Higher-Order Polynomial Chains & Soliton ALU Gates": They will view this as a form of topological temporal computing. "We are developing a non-von Neumann all-optical computing architecture that utilizes the momentum bandgaps (k-gaps) of photonic time crystals to handle continuous loss-compensation in soliton-based logic gates. Our initial models project an efficiency threshold exceeding 600 TOPS per watt, representing a 100x reduction in compute energy footprint compared to current CMOS/NVIDIA baselines.
